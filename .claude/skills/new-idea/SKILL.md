# New idea

1. Pick a short kebab-case slug from the idea.
2. Check `ideas/` and `projects/README.md` for something similar. If one exists, add to it instead of making a duplicate, and say so.
3. Create `ideas/<slug>.md` from `_templates/idea.md`, with today's date and status `raw`.
4. Fill in what the owner said in their own terms. Add your own "First thoughts": likely parts (check `components/inventory.md` and `research/tech-radar.md` first), the riskiest unknown, and a rough cost range.
5. Write a one-paragraph quick verdict: is it worth a full design review, and what would kill it?
6. Reply with the file link, the verdict, and offer `/design-review` as the next step.

Keep it quick. This is capture, not analysis.
